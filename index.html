Terran Explorer

A Digital Game-Based Learning Tool
for Earth Science Education

How to Play the Game

1. Search the Area
Explore the Earth and look for different areas such as the Sea, Volcano, Mountain, and Forest.
2. Check the Geological Features
Click or enter the different geological and physical features that you discover.
3. Explore the Area
Enter the selected area and look around to discover different objects and features.
4. Click the Objects
Click objects such as fish, shells, rocks, trees, lava, and other features to interact with them.
5. Learn the Fun Facts
Read the fun facts and explanations to learn more about Earth Science.
6. Explore and Learn
Continue exploring different areas and discover more information about the Earth and natural disasters.
