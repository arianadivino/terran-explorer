Definition

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known planet that supports life.

Explanation

It has oceans, land, atmosphere, and a dynamic crust that changes over time.

How to Play

  1. Move the character with the arrow keys or WASD.
  2. Explore the Earth to find Sea, Volcano, Mountain, and Forest regions.
  3. Click or tap geological and physical features to read definitions and fun facts.
  4. Enter different environments to investigate natural processes and landforms.
  5. Use the Back button to return to the Earth or Start Page whenever needed.
  6. Learn about natural disasters such as earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, tsunamis, and landslides.
